Hypothetical strategy replay — captured prices

Research simulation with hypothetical sizing. These results are separate from the recorded pilot ledger above and do not establish fills or actual profit.

Replay rule: select when estimated edge at the captured price (BUY uses the ask, SELL the bid; last trade only when no book was captured) ≥ 10%, size at 0.25× Kelly (cap 10% of simulated bankroll), compound a hypothetical $100000 bankroll. Quote availability does not guarantee execution. Filters: skip day-of (lead 0) trades · shrunk edge (walk-forward λ). Override with --edge-threshold / --kelly-fraction / --bankroll / --max-edge / --no-sell-buckets / --sell-only / --raw-edge.

Headline replay excludes trading fees and slippage. Fee treatment of comparison rows is stated separately.

Day-of (lead 0) trades are skipped by default: out-of-sample the market's intraday information beats the daemon's morning snapshot there decisively (Brier 0.050 vs 0.071). At lead ≥ 1 the model and market are at Brier parity in the ≥ 10¢ book — the tradeable edge is λ-shrunk selection within healthy segments, not global sharpness. --trade-day-of re-enables.
